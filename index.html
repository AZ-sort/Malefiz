ℹ️ About
Play Malefiz — a free online version of the classic Ravensburger board game.

How to Play

✕
🎯 Objective
🏆 Be the first player to move any one of your 5 pawns to the goal at the top of the board.
🎲 Taking a Turn
1️⃣ Roll the dice. You must move exactly the number shown — no more, no less.
2️⃣ Choose a pawn — either one already on the board, or deploy a new one from your home base.
3️⃣ Move it exactly that many steps. You can move in any direction and turn corners, but you cannot double back on the same move.
🚧 Barricades
🚫 You cannot pass through a barricade. You must go around, or land on it exactly to remove it.
✅ If you land exactly on a barricade, you remove it and get to place it anywhere on the board to block opponents. You cannot place it on the bottom row or the goal.
💥 Capturing
⚔️ If you land on a cell occupied by an opponent's pawn, their pawn is captured and sent back to their home base.
🤝 You can pass over any pawn — yours or an opponent's — without capturing it. Only landing on it counts.
🏠 Home Base
📦 Your 5 pawns start in your home base. To deploy one, simply select it and move — it enters the board at your entry point. Your dice roll counts from there.
🔄 Captured pawns return to your home base and can be redeployed on a future turn.

🎲 Double Roll

Roll two dice and move the higher number.
